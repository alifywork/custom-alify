01 Why is the pricing listed as “from”?

Because scope changes the number. The figures here are honest starting points for the described tier; a site with ten integrations and a forty-page catalogue sits well above them.

02 How do you keep a quote fixed?

We scope before we quote — pages, revisions, integrations and handover are listed line by line. If the scope holds, the price holds. Anything new is quoted before it is built.

03 What are the payment terms?

Typically 40% to start, 30% at design sign-off and the balance before launch. Retainers are billed monthly in advance, with no long lock-in.

04 Are there hidden costs?

No. Third-party costs — fonts, plugins, hosting, stock imagery or paid media — are listed separately and only bought once you approve them.

05 What if our scope grows mid-project?

Small adjustments inside the current phase are absorbed. Anything larger is quoted as a change and added to the list, so the launch date never slips silently.

06 Do you offer retainers instead of projects?

Yes. The Partner tier is a monthly retainer for ongoing design and development. It can start after a project or on its own, with a quarterly review.
