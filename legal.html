Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

01What this policy covers

This policy explains what information Alify collects when you use this website, why we collect it and the choices you have. It applies to alify.studio and to enquiries, quotes and project work that start here.

02Information you give us

When you send a form, request a quote or book a call we collect the details you type in: your name, email address, company, phone number, budget range and anything you write in the message field. We only ask for what we need to reply properly.

03Information we collect automatically

Our server logs the pages you request, the time of the request and your approximate location derived from your IP address. We use this to keep the site fast and to spot abuse. We do not build advertising profiles from it.

04Cookies and local storage

This site uses a small number of first-party cookies and local storage keys so preferences such as your last filter selection survive a page reload. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings; nothing essential breaks if you do.

05Analytics

If analytics are enabled, we use a privacy-friendly setup that records page views, referrers and device type without cross-site tracking. Analytics data is aggregated and never sold.

06How we use your information

We use your details to answer your enquiry, prepare a quote, deliver agreed work, send invoices, and — only if you opt in — share occasional studio updates. We never sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers.

07Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary with the processors that run our business: email hosting, project management, accounting and, where a project requires it, hosting or payment providers. Each is bound by a data-processing agreement.

08How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are deleted after twelve months. Project records, contracts and invoices are kept for seven years to meet tax and legal obligations. Analytics data is retained in aggregate for twenty-six months.

09Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to hello@alify.studio and we will respond within thirty days. If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority.

10Security

Access to client data is limited to the people working on your project. We use encrypted connections, password managers, two-factor authentication and regular backups. No system is perfect; if a breach ever affects your data we will tell you promptly.

11Children

This website is aimed at businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under sixteen. If you believe a child has sent us details, contact us and we will remove them.

12Changes to this policy

When we change how we handle data we update this page and revise the date at the top. Material changes are also announced in the studio newsletter.

Terms

Terms of service

Last updated 13 November 2026

01These terms

These terms govern the use of this website and any proposal, quote or project that follows from it. Where a signed statement of work exists, that document takes precedence over anything written here.

02Quotes and scope

Every quote is fixed for the scope described in it. Anything not written in the scope is not included. If the brief changes after approval we will price the difference in writing before doing the work.

03Payment

Projects are invoiced in milestones, typically fifty percent to start and the balance before launch. Retainers are invoiced monthly in advance. Invoices are payable within fourteen days. Work may be paused on overdue accounts.

04Timelines

Dates assume feedback within two working days at each review stage. If feedback is delayed the schedule moves by the same amount. We will always tell you early if a date is at risk.

05Your responsibilities

You agree to provide content, access and decisions on time, and to confirm that any material you supply — copy, images, fonts, trademarks — is licensed for the use you have asked for.

06Revisions

Each stage includes two rounds of revision unless the scope says otherwise. Further rounds are billed at the studio's hourly rate. Revisions do not include a change of direction after a stage has been approved.

07Intellectual property

On final payment you own the delivered designs, code and content created for you. We retain ownership of our pre-existing tools, libraries and internal frameworks, and we may show the work in our portfolio unless you ask us in writing not to.

08Confidentiality

Anything you share that is not public stays confidential. We will not disclose it to third parties without permission and we ask the same of our collaborators.

09Third-party services

Projects often rely on hosting, CMS, payment or analytics providers. Their terms and availability are outside our control. We will recommend sensible options, but licence and subscription costs are yours to hold.

10Cancellation

Either side may end an engagement with fourteen days' written notice. You pay for work completed up to that date plus any non-refundable third-party costs already committed. Deposits are non-refundable once discovery has started.

11Liability

We deliver work with reasonable skill and care. Our total liability for any claim is limited to the fees paid for the engagement in question. We are not liable for indirect losses such as lost profit or lost opportunity.

12Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Pakistan, and the courts of Karachi have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute. We will always try to resolve a disagreement directly first.
