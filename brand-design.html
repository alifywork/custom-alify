Overview

A brand people
can describe.

If your team describes the brand three different ways, the problem is not the logo. It is the system behind it.

We start with positioning — who it is for, what it promises, how it sounds — then design the marks and rules that express it. The result is one page your team can repeat, not a folder of options.

Every identity is tested in the places it will actually be seen: a phone screen, a favicon, an invoice, a shop window.

What's included

From positioning
to rollout.

An identity engagement is three stages, listed here in full. Production of packaging or signage is quoted separately.

Stage 01

Foundation

Stage 02

Identity

  • Primary and secondary marks

Stage 03

Fonts are licensed to you directly. Photography, packaging and signage production are quoted separately.

Good to know

Identity
questions.

What clients ask before a rebrand.

Still deciding?

Send the brief you have — even a rough one. We will tell you what this service would actually involve.

Get a quote Ask a question
01 Can we keep our existing logo?

Yes. If the mark works we refine the system around it — type, colour and layout usually carry more weight than the logo does.

02 How many logo options do we see?

Two or three directions, each shown in real applications. More options do not make the decision easier.

03 Do we own the files?

Everything transfers on final payment: source files, exports, fonts where licensing allows, and the guidelines.

04 What if we need packaging?

Packaging design is quoted separately once the identity is settled, and usually takes two to three weeks.

05 Will the brand work online?

Every identity is tested on a screen first — favicon, social crops, site header — because that is where most people meet it.

06 How long does it take?

Three to six weeks for an identity system. Templates and rollout assets add a week or two.
