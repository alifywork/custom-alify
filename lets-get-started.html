A good brief

Four things worth having ready.

  • The goalWhat the business needs this to do, in one or two sentences.
  • The audienceWho it is for, and what they need to decide or do.
  • The timingAny dates you are working towards, even rough ones.
  • The budgetA range is enough — it decides scope, not the other way round.
