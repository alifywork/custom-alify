Our story

Built on curiosity, kept honest by craft.

Alify began with a simple frustration: too much digital work looks impressive and does very little. We wanted a studio where strategy, design and engineering sit at the same table from day one.

A decade later that is still the shape of the studio. Senior people stay on the work, decisions are explained in plain language, and nothing ships until it earns its place on the page.

“Good work gets easier to defend when everyone can see the thinking.”— The Alify team, studio principle 01

Milestones

  1. 2016

    Two designers and a developer, one rented desk and a stubborn belief in doing the thinking first.

  2. 2020

    The studio grows into a full product team — research, interface design, engineering and growth under one roof.

  3. Today

    A small senior team working with founders and marketing leads across fourteen countries.

Written by the studio, updated quarterly.

Working style

Predictable rhythm, no theatre.

The same rhythm on every project — whether it runs six weeks or six months.

How we work

  • Start with the problem

    A short discovery before a single screen is drawn.

  • Show work early

    Rough ideas in week one beats a polished surprise in week six.

  • Ship in small pieces

    Each release is usable, tested and easy to roll back.

  • Hand over properly

    Documentation, files and training so your team keeps moving.

What we expect from each other

  • Clear decisions

    One decision-maker on your side keeps feedback sharp and fast.

  • Honest timelines

    We say what fits in the time, even when that means less scope.

  • Direct feedback

    Disagreement is welcome early; it saves weeks later.

  • Shared context

    Access to analytics, brand assets and the people who know the history.
