Our process

Good work is a process.
Let’s make it clear.

Six clear phases from the first conversation to post-launch support — fixed scope, weekly demos and no surprises along the way.

The timeline

Six phases.
No guesswork.

Each phase has one job, a fixed outcome and a decision we make together. You always know what is happening this week and what comes next.

Typical build4–8 weeks
Your time2–3 hrs / week
Book a kickoff call
  1. 01

    Week 1

    Discover

    We start with the business, not the pixels: goals, audience, competitors and what success actually looks like in numbers.

    Outcome: Brief agreed and success metrics written down.

  2. 02
    02

    Strategy

    Positioning, page structure and a measurement plan. This is where we agree the single idea the site has to land — before anything visual.

    03

    Wireframes first, then the visual system. You see real pages in sequence, not mood boards, and we refine them in two structured rounds.

    04

    The front-end build on the agreed CMS. Responsive at every breakpoint, with performance, accessibility and SEO handled inside the build, not bolted on.

    Outcome: Staging site live for your review.

  5. 05
    05

    Launch

    QA, redirects, analytics, DNS and a go-live checklist. We launch in a quiet window and watch the first 48 hours closely.

    Outcome: Site live, tracking verified, access handed over.

    Support

    Thirty days of included support after launch, then optional care: monitoring, updates, small improvements and a monthly report.

    Outcome: Your team confident, or a care plan running.

Working together

  • A demo every weekYou see the work live each Thursday, not a status email.
  • One senior leadThe person who scopes your project also designs it.

All FAQs Book a call
01 How long does a project take?

A focused marketing site runs four to eight weeks from kickoff. Larger builds, ecommerce or product work take eight to fourteen, depending on content and how fast approvals come back.

02 Who is our main point of contact?

A senior designer or strategist leads your project and stays with it from kickoff to launch. You get their direct Slack and email — no account-manager relay.

03 How often will we see progress?

Every week. We demo live work on Thursdays and the staging link is always available, so nothing appears for the first time at launch.

04 What if we need changes mid-project?

Small adjustments inside the current phase are absorbed. Anything that changes the agreed scope is quoted and added to a change list, so the launch date does not silently slip.

05 Where does communication happen?

Day-to-day questions and files live in your Slack channel. Formal approvals, quotes and scope changes are confirmed by email so there is always a record.

06 What happens after launch?

Thirty days of support is included for fixes and small changes. After that, most clients move onto a monthly care plan or book work as needed.
