16 questions

01 What services do you offer?

Brand and identity, website design and build, product and UX work, and ongoing growth support. Most clients start with one and end up using two or three.

02 Can you refresh an existing brand instead of starting again?

Often, yes. If the core idea still holds we tighten the identity, the site and the messaging rather than throwing away what already has recognition.

03 Do you build on WordPress and Shopify?

Both, plus custom front-ends when the project needs it. We recommend the platform after we understand how your team will actually maintain the site.

04 Do you work with in-house design teams?

Frequently. We can lead, partner alongside your team, or hand over a system your designers keep running after launch.

05 What does a typical project cost?

Most engagements land between $2,400 and $6,800. Larger product builds run higher. Every quote is fixed before we begin.

06 How do you quote a project?

We scope in a short paid discovery: goals, pages, complexity and timeline. You get a fixed price with a clear list of what is included.

07 Can we pay in instalments?

Yes. Typically 40% to start, 30% at the design sign-off and the balance before launch. Retainers are billed monthly.

08 Are there any hidden costs?

No. Third-party costs like fonts, plugins, hosting or stock imagery are listed separately and approved by you before we buy anything.

09 How long does a website project take?

A focused marketing site takes four to eight weeks. Larger builds or product work run eight to fourteen, depending on content and approvals.

10 Who will I work with day to day?

A senior designer or strategist leads your project and stays with it from kickoff to launch. No account-manager relay race.

11 How many revision rounds are included?

Two structured rounds at each stage. In practice most decisions are settled in the first round because we agree the direction before designing.

12 What do you need from us to start?

A decision-maker in the room, honest context about the business, and access to whatever exists today. We handle the rest of the discovery.

13 What happens after launch?

Thirty days of included support for fixes and small adjustments, plus a handover session so your team knows how everything works.

14 Can we update the site ourselves?

Yes. Every build ships with an editable content structure and a short recording walkthrough for the pages you will touch most.

15 Do you help with hosting and domains?

We can set up hosting, DNS and email, or work with your existing provider. Either way you keep ownership of every account.

16 What if something breaks later?

Care plans cover monitoring, updates and priority fixes. Clients without a plan can still book work at an hourly rate.
