What happens next

The next few days, mapped out.

No black box. Here is exactly what we do with your message, and when.

  1. 01
    01
    Today

    We read it

    A person opens your message the same day, reads the whole thing and checks whether anything is missing before replying.

    You get: You get a reply from a human, not an autoresponder.

  2. 02
    02
    Within 1 working day

    A proper answer

    From the senior lead who would run the project: honest thoughts on scope, timing and whether we are the right fit.

    You get: Either a clear next step, or a straight no — both useful.

  3. 03
    03
    Within 48 hours of the call

    Scope call and fixed quote

    If it makes sense to continue, we book 30 minutes, pressure-test the scope and send a line-item fixed quote.

    You get: A price you can plan a budget around, in writing.
