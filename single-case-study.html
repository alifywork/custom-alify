Case study

Helping a growing team
find its next chapter.

A six-month rebuild of brand, store and site — and what changed after launch.

Get a quoteAll services
Client
Northstar
Goal
Make the product understandable in one screen
Timeline
8 weeks, Feb – Apr 2026
Services
Brand identity, Web design, Front-end build

The challenge

Four products,
one confused buyer.

Northstar had outgrown its own story. Each team — product, sales, support — described the platform differently, and the website carried all four versions at once.

In discovery we read a month of sales calls and found the same eleven questions repeated. Prospects were not asking about features; they were asking what Northstar replaced.

The brief was a redesign. The work was a decision: one sentence, agreed by the founders, that every page would have to support.

Discuss a similar project All case studies

  • What was at stake

    A Series A raise and a first real sales team. If buyers could not repeat what Northstar did, every call started from zero.

  • What was not working

    Nine pages, four product names and a homepage that described the company instead of the customer’s problem.

  • What we had to protect

    An existing CMS the team liked, an editorial blog with five years of traffic, and a launch date tied to a conference.

The approach

Four stages,
eight weeks.

Each stage ended with something approved, so the next one never argued about the last.

  1. 01
    01
    Week 1–2

    Audit & interviews

    Six stakeholder interviews, a read of sales calls and a full crawl of the existing site. We mapped where the story changed from page to page.

    Outcome: One page of agreed positioning.

  2. 02
    02
    Week 2–3

    Structure

    Sitemap rebuilt around the buyer’s decision order: what it replaces, who it is for, what it costs, how to start.

    Outcome: Eight pages, each with one job.

  3. 03
    03
    Week 3–5

    Design system

    Six components and two type sizes, designed mobile-first and mapped one-to-one onto the existing CMS so nothing needed rebuilding.

    Outcome: Approved templates and component library.

  4. 04
    04
    Week 5–8

    Build & launch

    Front-end build on the existing stack, content entered by the team, redirects mapped from all nine old pages.

    Outcome: A live site and a CMS the team runs.

The metrics

What changed
after launch.

Six months of data against the same period a year earlier.

  • +46% Improved Qualified demo requests First ninety days after launch, on unchanged ad spend.
  • −38% Reduced Support tickets Fewer “where do I start” emails reaching the team.
  • 1.2s Improved First contentful paint Mid-range Android on 4G, third-party scripts included.
  • −41% Reduced Sales call length Fewer basics to re-explain before the real conversation.
