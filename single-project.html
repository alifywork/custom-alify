Overview & challenge

A fragmented story,
told five ways.

Northstar had a good product and a website that made it sound like four products. Sales calls were spent re-explaining the basics.

The brief asked for a new site. The honest answer was that the site was a symptom: the internal narrative had drifted, and every team had written its own version.

We spent the first two weeks in the business rather than in Figma — interviews, a read of the sales calls and a crawl of every page — and came back with one page everyone could agree on.

Read the case study All projects

  • The challenge

    Five people inside the company described the product five different ways — and the website repeated all five.

  • What we found

    Visitors could not tell what Northstar actually replaced, so they could not decide whether to book a demo.

  • The constraint

    Eight weeks to launch, an existing CMS to keep, and no budget for new photography.

The solution

Say one thing,
then prove it everywhere.

Three moves, in this order, over eight weeks. Each one reduced what the next stage had to argue about.

See it up close
01

One page of position

Who it is for, what it replaces and the sentence every page has to support. Agreed with the founders in a single session.

02

A system, not pages

Six components and two type sizes rebuilt from scratch, with the CMS mapped to them so new pages stay on-brand.

03

Someone measured it

Performance and accessibility budgets agreed before design, checked on a mid-range phone, and paid for in the scope.
