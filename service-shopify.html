Shopify overview

Merchandising first,
then the theme.

A store is a sales surface. We structure collections, product pages and checkout around how people actually decide to buy.

We start with the catalogue: how products group, what a shopper needs at each step, and where the current store loses them.

Then we build the theme around that structure, so merchandising changes do not need a developer.

Start a project Read a case study

  • Shoppers decide faster

    Product pages answer the three questions that stall a purchase: fit, delivery and returns.

  • Cleaner catalogue

    Collections, tags and metafields are planned so filters and search return useful results.

  • Checkout without surprises

    Costs, options and delivery timings appear before the payment step, which protects conversion.

  • Measurable from day one

    Analytics, events and a test backlog are set up at launch, not months later.

What store owners ask before a rebuild.

Still deciding?

Send the brief you have — even a rough one. We will tell you what this service would actually involve.

Get a quote Ask a question
01 Do we keep our existing orders and customers?

Yes. Products, customers, orders and reviews migrate across, and we map redirects so old product URLs keep their rankings.

02 Can our team edit the store after launch?

The theme is built from sections, so product ranges, banners and campaign pages can change without touching code.

03 Do you work with Shopify Plus?

Yes, including multi-currency, B2B catalogues and custom checkout extensions where Plus is needed.

04 How long does a rebuild take?

Four to eight weeks for a typical catalogue. Migration and photography are usually the deciding factors, not theme work.

05 What happens after launch?

Thirty days of support is included for fixes and small changes. After that, a retainer covers new pages and improvements.

06 Can we start smaller?

Yes — a landing page or a single-template redesign is a normal first step, and often the honest way to test the working relationship.
