Three things worth having ready

  • The one outcome that mattersNot a wish list — the single result you need.

The agenda

Thirty minutes, planned minute by minute.

You will know what we are covering before you join, and what you leave with.

  1. 00:00

    Intros and context

    Who is in the room, what your business does and why this came up now.

  2. 05:00

    The goal

    What the project has to achieve, who it is for and how you would measure it.

  3. 15:00

    Scope and constraints

    Pages, platforms, integrations, content and the deadline you are working to.

  4. 25:00

    Honest next step

    A recommendation, a rough range and the three things we would do first — in writing.
