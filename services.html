Questions about
working with us.

The practical things clients ask before choosing a service. Anything else, ask directly.

Not sure which service?

Describe the problem — we will tell you what it actually needs, even if that is less than you expected.

Get a quote Ask a question
01 Can we start with just one service?

Yes. Most clients begin with a single piece — a site, a brand or a technical SEO fix — and add the rest once it is earning its keep.

02 Do you build on WordPress and Shopify?

Both, and custom front-ends where they fit better. We recommend the platform after we understand how your team will maintain the site.

03 Who actually does the work?

A small senior team: strategy, design and build in-house. We bring in specialists for photography or paid media, and tell you before we do.

04 What if we already have a designer or developer?

Then we work alongside them. We can lead, partner or hand over a system your team runs afterwards — plenty of projects are a mix.

05 How long does a website take?

Four to eight weeks for a focused marketing site. Larger builds, stores or product work run eight to fourteen weeks depending on content and approvals.

06 Can we start with an audit?

Yes. A paid audit of the site, brand or search performance is often the cheapest way to decide what is worth doing next.
